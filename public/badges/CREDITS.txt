Game-style badge artwork generated by the owner.
Each file is a centre crop (88%, eggs 90%) of a 1024px original, resized to 320px webp,
then turned into pixel art with `python3 scripts/pixelate-badge.py public/badges/<id>.webp` (64x64 grid, 48 colours, lossless).
Run the script once per new badge, on the fresh 320px crop.
